GoAwayBCA Beta 1 scope

Windows 8.1, 10 and 11 installation to existing internal GPT exFAT/NTFS partitions; pure EFI booting; driver downloads; installation management; Windows boot-menu maintenance; EFI access; Saved Files cleanup; diagnostics and experimental boot recovery.
Windows 7 and 8 are not yet supported and are being actively developed for a future update.
No partition creation/resizing, hybrid MBR support, USB destinations, partition backup/restore, Leopard or 32-bit EFI support is included.
See README.txt for requirements, tested configurations, precautions and limitations.
